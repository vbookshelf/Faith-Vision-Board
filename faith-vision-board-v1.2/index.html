How it works

Using your board

  • Click a card to enlarge it.
  • Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to move a card to any column, including empty spaces.
  • Use the buttons on a card's top right to edit or remove it.
  • While adding or editing a card, click "+ Add Scripture" to include more than one verse.
  • Click the main title or subtitle to rename the vision board.

Saved locally, never in the cloud

Your images, scriptures and notes are saved only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is uploaded or sent to the cloud, and no account is needed. If you add a photo by web link, only the link is saved; the picture is loaded from that website.

Using a different browser or device

Because nothing is stored online, your vision board will not appear if you open this page in a different browser, a private window, or on another device. Clearing your browser data, or choosing Delete All, also removes it.

Keep a copy

Use Save as Picture from the ⋯ menu to download a png image of your whole board.
